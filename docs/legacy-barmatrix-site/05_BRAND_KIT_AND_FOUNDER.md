## 2. Founder story & method origin (`about.html`)

Canonical first-person narrative (old brand persona: **Vera Brooks**, 11 years CA bar tutoring, 600+ students). The story is the origin of the trap-taxonomy insight ("the shapes of wrong answers"), naming the families: **Stale Rule, Wrong Timing, Channel Mismatch, Decisionmaker Inversion, Modal Inversion, Purpose Poisoning**.

**5-step timeline:** 2015 first cohort → 2018 the spreadsheet → 2021 the 47 traps → 2024 focus group (n=1,247) → 2026 v1 launch. Stat line: 11 years / 600+ students / 47 traps / 156 tension points.

> The *narrative arc* (tutor notices wrong answers repeat → builds a spreadsheet → formalizes a taxonomy → validates with a focus group → ships) is a strong, reusable About-page structure. The specific persona name/bio is swappable per the rebrand.

- **5 fully-worked trap cards**, each with a real FRE-anchored misconception + focus-group A/B/C/D bar distribution + miss rate + tension point + assigned repair drill:
  1. Decisionmaker Inversion / FRE 104(a)
  2. Rules-Bound Trap / judge not bound by rules
  3. Modal Inversion / FRE 104(b) sufficiency vs preponderance
  4. Channel Mismatch / FRE 104(c) confession hearing outside jury
  5. Purpose Poisoning / FRE 105 limited-purpose
- **8×7 personal Tension Matrix** heatmap (8 MBE subjects × 7 trap dimensions: Rule/Exc, Timing, Party, Scope, Standard, Triggers, Remedy).
- "Your Next 67 Days" 3-phase repair path.
