▌ GETTING STARTED

What do I do tonight, right after enrolling?

+
Take the diagnostic if you haven't. Then open your dashboard — your Today's Queue is already loaded with Drill A1 (Roles of Judge and Jury) and a Privileges drill behind it. 30 minutes is the right session length. Don't try to do all 32 questions on day one.

How do I install the iOS app?

+
Open your dashboard sidebar → Mobile Apps. The TestFlight install link will be emailed to you. Install TestFlight first if you don't have it. Same account logs in everywhere.

What's the difference between a Red-Zone Drill and a Boot Camp?

+
Red-Zone Drills are 8-12 questions targeting one tension point (e.g. "Decisionmaker Inversion"). Boot Camps are 5-day sequences of drills bundled by subject. Both queue automatically based on your misses.
▌ BILLING & REFUNDS

Can I switch from pay-in-full to payment plan?

+
Yes. Email billing@barmatrix.app within the first 3 days. We'll refund the difference and re-bill at the plan rate ($450 + $449/30d if you're inside founding cohort, otherwise $500 + $499).

How do I request a refund?

+
Within 3 days of purchase: reply to your receipt email, or email billing@barmatrix.app. No questions asked. Funds return to your card in 5-10 business days. After 3 days, refunds are case-by-case.

Where's my receipt?

+
Auto-emailed to the address on file within 60 seconds of purchase. You can also re-download anytime: open the app → Account → Billing → Download PDF Receipt.
▌ MOBILE APPS

iOS and Android availability?

+
Web access is live now. iOS is in TestFlight (private testing) while public App Store review clears. Android is in Play testing track. Same login. Same drills. Public store availability tracking via app status banner.

Will my progress sync across web/iOS/Android?

+
Yes. Start a drill on web at lunch, finish it on iOS on the train, see the mastery update on Android at night. Real-time sync across all three.
▌ CONTENT QUESTIONS

I think a wrong-answer explanation is incorrect — what do I do?

+
Report it. Open the question in the dashboard, click the "Flag" icon, and tell us what you think is wrong. Every flag is reviewed within 48 hours. If you're right, the question is corrected for everyone and you get credit.

Are these official NCBE questions?

+
No. BarMatrix uses original MBE-style questions written by our team. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NCBE or any bar authority.

Where are the California distinctions called out?

+
Every question with a CA flag carries a separate explanation lane for the California Evidence Code analog. You'll see it in the forensics overlay after answering — look for the "▌ CA DISTINCTION" panel.
