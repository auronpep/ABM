Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

▌ Effective date: June 11, 2026

What We Collect

Email address

We collect the email address you provide at checkout or when contacting support. We use it to send your receipt, enrollment confirmation, and any transactional messages related to your account.

Study activity

We collect your diagnostic answers, drill attempts, and results. This data is used to build your Red-Zone map and personalized study plan — the core of how BarMatrix works. Without it, the diagnostic and adaptive queuing cannot function.

Local progress data

Progress data — your session state, mastery levels, and queue position — is stored locally in your browser's localStorage on the device where you study. This data stays on your device. If you clear your browser data on that device, that local progress is cleared. Moving to a new device or browser requires a fresh start on local data.

Visit and analytics data

We collect basic visit data including page views and UTM campaign parameters (the source/medium/campaign tags in URLs). This helps us understand which marketing efforts are working. We do not collect device fingerprints or track you across sites.

Payments

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's hosted checkout page. BarMatrix never receives or stores your card number or full payment credentials. Stripe's privacy policy governs how payment data is handled. We receive a confirmation of purchase and the email address associated with it.

Transactional Email

Receipts and enrollment confirmations may be sent via our email service provider. We do not send marketing email without your consent beyond transactional messages directly related to your purchase.

How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to:

  • Deliver and operate the BarMatrix study platform
  • Generate your diagnostic results, Red-Zone map, and study queue
  • Send receipts and account-related transactional messages
  • Understand how users reach us (UTM analytics)
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell personal data.

Data Retention

We retain your email address and study activity data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward in case you need support or reactivation. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

Your Rights

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. To submit a request, email support@barmatrix.app with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within a reasonable time and fulfill valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of BarMatrix after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@barmatrix.app.
