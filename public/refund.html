Refund Policy

The complete, canonical refund policy for BarMatrix purchases.

▌ Effective date: June 11, 2026
3-day window.
No questions asked. From the date of purchase. Email billing@barmatrix.app to request.

The 3-Day Window

You have 3 days from the date of purchase to request a full refund, no questions asked. The window is 3 calendar days starting from the date and time your purchase was completed.

To request a refund within the window, do either of the following:

  • Reply directly to the receipt email you received at purchase
  • Email billing@barmatrix.app with your order details

Refunds return to the original payment method. Processing time is typically 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

After the 3-Day Window

After the 3-day window closes, refund requests are handled case-by-case. Email billing@barmatrix.app and we will review your situation. We do not guarantee refunds outside the window, but we will respond to every request.

The 2-Pay Plan

BarMatrix Flagship is also available on a 2-pay plan: $500 charged at purchase and $499 charged automatically approximately 30 days later.

If you purchased on the 2-pay plan and request a refund within the 3-day window:

  • The refund covers the amounts actually charged up to that point (typically just the first installment of $500)
  • The remaining unpaid installment is cancelled — it will not be charged

If the second installment has already been charged before you request a refund, both installments are covered within the 3-day window from the original purchase date.

Switching Between Plans

Within the first 3 days of purchase, you may switch between the pay-in-full option ($999) and the 2-pay plan ($500 + $499). Email billing@barmatrix.app to request the switch. We will handle the billing adjustment.

How Refunds Are Processed

Refunds are processed via Stripe back to the original payment method used at checkout. We do not issue refunds to a different card or via alternative methods. Timing is controlled by your card issuer — most refunds post within 5–10 business days, though it can occasionally take longer.

Contact

General support: support@barmatrix.app.
