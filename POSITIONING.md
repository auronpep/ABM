# POSITIONING

*Who the program is for and how we talk about it. Answers to [VISION.md](VISION.md).*

---

## Who it's for

Bible-believing Christian bar takers. We build for them and optimize for them — not for the widest possible audience. Narrowing the audience is the strategy, not a cost of it.

## The stance: offensively Christian

The site is overtly, unapologetically Christian. Scripture and faith are **identity**, not decoration — woven in, not bolted on.

**The bounce test:** a neutral, conservative, non-Christian Republican should land on the site, feel *"this isn't for me,"* and leave. **That is the design working, not a bug.** If our copy is mild enough that this person feels comfortable, we have failed in the other direction.

## Never gate for being "too Christian" or "too conservative"

This is a standing instruction to everyone who writes copy here, **including AI:**

- Do not soften, hedge, qualify, or add disclaimers to make the faith content more palatable.
- Do not "balance" it for broader appeal.
- Do not flag faith or conservative content as a risk to manage. It isn't one. It's the point.

The only thing we tune for palatability is nothing. Write it for our people.

## Marketing resets to zero

None of the old bar material or old positioning carries forward. Scaffolding and plumbing stay; the message starts blank. Every claim is re-earned from this document set, not inherited.

## Sell the outcome, not the engine

The engine — distillation, the keys, the thesis, the playbook strategy — is *why* the product is good. It isn't the pitch. We sell what the student gets and don't narrate the machinery. That's a positioning choice, not a secret: we don't pitch it, and we don't hedge about not pitching it either.

## Voice

Direct, certain, and scripture-integrated. We point; we don't waffle. The product's whole philosophy is *take a side* — the copy carries the same spine.

Specific locked copy from the existing build (headline, hero scripture, naming) is **carried over and reviewable** — it is not re-litigated here. This document governs the *stance*; specific lines are a build-level decision.
